Store policies

Effective November 21, 2026. Questions: tacticalaminos@gmail.com.

Shipping and payment

We ship within the United States using USPS Ground Advantage. Shipping is $8 per order, as shown at checkout. Orders are prepared after payment is confirmed. An order confirmation acknowledges your order; it is not a payment receipt.

Pay only using the instructions in your order email. Contact us if you need help or believe you paid the wrong amount. USPS delivery estimates are estimates, not guarantees. We email tracking when an order is marked shipped; tracking may take time to update.

If we cannot ship within an advertised timeframe, or within 30 days when no timeframe was provided, we will contact you with a revised estimate and the choice to accept the delay or cancel for a prompt refund of the unshipped items. Contact us promptly about a missing delivery so we can investigate.

Cancellations, returns and refunds

Contact us with your order number as soon as possible to request cancellation or an address correction. We can cancel an unpaid order. For paid orders that have not shipped, contact us to arrange cancellation and a refund. Once shipped, an address change or cancellation may no longer be possible.

For damaged, incorrect or missing items, email your order number and a description of the issue, with photos when available. Keep the product and packaging while we review the issue. We will work with you on an appropriate replacement or refund.

Do not send products back without contacting us first. Opened or used products are not accepted for change-of-mind returns. Contact us about unopened products before returning them; eligibility and return shipping will be explained before you agree to a return. These policies do not limit rights that apply under law.

Approved refunds are sent through the original payment method when possible. We will confirm when a refund is issued; the payment provider controls when it appears in your account.

Privacy

We collect information you provide when creating an account, saving an address, placing an order or contacting us. This includes your name, email, phone number, shipping address, order details and communications. We use it to operate your account, fulfill orders, provide support, prevent misuse and maintain business records.

Our service providers include Google Firebase for account and database services, Vercel for website hosting, Resend for order emails, USPS for shipping, and Venmo or PayPal for payments. Relevant information is shared with providers as needed to deliver these services. Payment credentials are handled by your payment provider, not collected in our checkout form.

Your browser stores sign-in information and session cart data to support account access and checkout. Account and order information is available to authorized store administrators. We retain information as needed for service, security, business records and applicable obligations. No online system can guarantee complete security.

You can update your email or password and remove your saved address under My account. Email us for other access, correction or deletion requests. Some order or business records may need to be retained. Provider services have their own privacy practices. We may update this policy; the current version will be available here.
